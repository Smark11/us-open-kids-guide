At 768 only the "Grandstand · On now"/"Armstrong · On now" chips clip (169px vs 155px). Fix: at narrow widths drop the court name when a status exists (`✅ Done` / `🔴 On now`, court stays in the sheet), or wrap the text (`white-space: normal; display: inline-block; max-width: none`) — the `🏁 Fri` and `📅 Sun` chips are fine.
