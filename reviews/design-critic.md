# design-critic review — iteration 1
Verdict: FAIL

Tested at 390×844 and 768×1024 via Playwright (chromium-1228), both `http://localhost:8765/us-open-kids-guide/` and `file:///…/index.html` (file:// renders identically: 32 cards, 0 broken images, 0 console errors). Screenshots: scratchpad `review-shots/design-critic/` (phone-*/tablet-* 00–17). Fonts (Lilita One, Nunito 700/800/900) confirmed loaded; no horizontal overflow at either width (scrollWidth == viewport).

Overall: this is emphatically NOT a Bootstrap/Tailwind template. Court blue + cream + ball yellow, Lilita One headings with hard ink shadows, 4px borders, tilted cards, the ball seed badge and the star pop are distinctive and joyful. Contrast is excellent everywhere (ink on cream, white on blue, no grey, no thin weights). The failures below are layout/hierarchy bugs, not direction.

## Blocking (must fix)
- [B1] styles/main.css `.ball-bounce` / `.hero-kicker` @390: the bouncing ball (`right:4%; top:-2px`, 44px wide) lands on top of the right end of the "32 PLAYERS · 16 MATCHES" kicker pill (measured ball.left=319 < kicker.right=324; visible overlap in phone-01). It is the very first thing on the page. → Move the ball out of the kicker's row: e.g. `.ball-bounce{ top: 8px; right: 8px }` and add `.hero-kicker{ max-width: calc(100% - 64px) }`, or drop the ball to sit beside the date line (`top: 50%`) where there is empty blue.
- [B2] index.html/app.js `#hero-title` @390: the title wraps as "Nora's US / Open Guide", splitting the brand "US Open" across lines and burying the kid's name inside line 1. The question "is Nora's US Open Guide the star?" is a qualified yes on tablet and no on phone: nothing about "Nora's" is special — same white as the rest, and the yellow kicker above it out-shouts it. → Render the title as two explicit lines: `<span class="hero-name">Nora's</span><br>US Open Guide` (renderHero builds this), keep "US Open Guide" `white-space:nowrap` at ≥360px (fits at 42px), and style `.hero-name` as the sticker: ball-yellow (`--ball`) with the same ink text-shadow, `transform: rotate(-2deg)`, slightly larger. Shrink the kicker to a plain white 16px line under the date so it stops competing.
- [B3] styles/main.css `.match` / `.match-row` @390 and @768: every match card stacks player / lone "vs" badge / player in one column, so the "vs" floats at the left with ~60% of the cream card empty (phone-02, tablet-02). Sixteen of these are ~230px tall each, which pushes the sticker album — the whole concept — to y=4,315px on the phone (5 full screens of scrolling; footer at 10,684). Hierarchy reads hero → wall of matches → players. → Compact the match card: `.match-row{ grid-template-columns: 1fr auto 1fr; }` at ≥600px with `.vs` in the middle column; at 390 keep stacked but put the `.vs` badge in the gutter (`position:absolute; left:50%; transform:translate(-50%,-50%)` between the two rows, or `grid-template-columns: auto 1fr` with vs spanning) and cut `.match-player{min-height:44px}` / `.match{padding:8px 10px}` so a card is ≤150px. Also add a yellow "See the player cards ⬇" pill in the hero (`href="#players"`) so Nora can jump straight to the album.
- [B4] styles/main.css `.filters` sticky @≥700: the sticky bar is 178px tall today (3 pill rows) and will be ~290px on Saturday when court + session rows render; its background is a gradient that fades to transparent, so mid-scroll the pills float over card photos and the muted "Court filters unlock…" pill sits directly on top of card faces (tablet-04b). Seed badges and photo tops peek through the fade. → Either (a) make only the first row sticky and give it a solid `--court` background with a `4px solid var(--ink)` bottom rule, or (b) drop `position:sticky` entirely (the filters are one scroll-up away). If sticky stays, no gradient: solid background + hard bottom edge, and cap it to one horizontally-scrolling row (`display:flex; overflow-x:auto; flex-wrap:nowrap`).

## Should fix
- [S1] Type sizes below the 18px sun rule (DESIGN.md "Body ≥ 18px on phone"): `.tag` 14px (tier/draw chips on every card and match), `.match-meta` 15px, `.card-tag` 15px (the taglines under names), `.stat .k` 13px, `.src-list` 15px, `.verified` 14px, `.footer` 16px. All are bold and high-contrast, so they pass contrast, but 14–15px chips are the smallest text a 9-year-old will read outdoors. → `.tag{font-size:16px; padding:4px 12px}`, `.card-tag{font-size:17px}`, `.stat .k{font-size:14px}`, `.footer{font-size:17px}`.
- [S2] `.flip` / `.face` fun-fact geometry: faces are `position:absolute; inset:0` in a fixed 140px box, so a long fact cannot grow the card. At 768 (3-col, ~230px wide) three facts overflow their border by 12px: Coco Gauff #5, Amanda Anisimova #2, Zheng Qinwen #5 (scrollHeight 144 > 132). → Stack the faces with grid instead of absolute: `.flip-inner{display:grid} .face{grid-area:1/1; position:static}` — the box then takes the taller face's height and the 3D flip still works. Also `min-height:160px`.
- [S3] `.flip:hover .flip-inner{rotateY(12deg)}` and `[aria-pressed=true]:hover{rotateY(168deg)}`: on iPad/iPhone hover state sticks after a tap, so the flipped fact stays visibly skewed (tablet-13 shows the yellow back face tilted). → Wrap both hover rules in `@media (hover:hover)`.
- [S4] Fun-fact front face is a flat purple slab reading "Fun fact #2 / Tap to flip! 🔄" in 16px; on the phone seven identical slabs stack into a purple wall (phone-12). The flip itself is smooth and the yellow back is a nice reveal, but the front does not look like something to flip. → Make the front a sealed sticker: a huge "?" in Lilita (48px) or the 🤫 emoji, `.face.front .tap` 18px, cycle front colors through `--t1/--t2/--t3` by index, and add a folded-corner cue (`::after` triangle in `--paper` at bottom-right). On phone, 2 columns of shorter facts (`minmax(160px,1fr)`) would halve the wall.
- [S5] Detail `.stats` "Tier" cell: the emoji sits alone on a line above "Superstars" (because `<small>` is `display:block`), and the cell is orphaned at half width in its own row on both viewports (phone-11, tablet-10). → Drop the Tier stat (the tier chip is already on the card and in the match box) or render it as `<span class="tag t1">🔥 Superstars</span>` inside a `.stat.wide`.
- [S6] `.d-nav .pill .nm` @390 truncates names: "◀ Elena Ryba…", "Cristina Buc…". → Show first name only on ≤600px (`prev.name.split(' ')[0]`), or let the pill wrap to two 17px lines.
- [S7] `.pill.muted` "🏟️ Court filters unlock Saturday morning!" looks like a disabled button, and at 390 it wraps with a hanging indent under the emoji (phone-03). → Make it plain white text in `.count` style ("Court + time filters appear Saturday morning") rather than a pill, or shorten to "🏟️ Courts: Saturday AM".
- [S8] `.hero-tip` @390: the demo seed badge ① wraps alone onto its own line after "star!" (phone-01). → Put the badge inside a `white-space:nowrap` span with "star!" or move it to the start of the sentence.
- [S9] Card grid ragged bottoms @390: 14 names and 17 taglines wrap to two lines, so tilted cards in the same row end at different heights (Zverev 345px vs Rybakina 322px). Sticker-album charm covers some of this, but rows like Andreeva/Świątek look uneven. → `.card-tag{display:-webkit-box; -webkit-line-clamp:1; overflow:hidden}` or give `.card-body` a `min-height` that fits two name lines.
- [S10] `.d-close` is `position:fixed` under 900px, so while scrolling the detail it sits on top of content (covers the Seed "#4" value in phone-11, the story box in tablet-13). Acceptable as a floating control, but → give it a thicker solid ring (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 4px var(--paper), var(--shadow)`) so it never visually merges with what is under it.

## Nice to have
- [N1] Detail `.d-country` repeats the country: "🇺🇸 United States · from Delray Beach, Florida, United States". Strip a trailing ", <countryName>" from hometown.
- [N2] The hero kicker "32 PLAYERS · 16 MATCHES" is the only ball-yellow element in the hero and sits above the title, so the eye lands on it first. Once B2 makes "Nora's" yellow, demote the kicker to white text.
- [N3] Body court lines (`repeating-linear-gradient` centre line at 18% white) are nearly invisible under the card grid; the hero's two lines read well. Either commit (add the baseline/sidelines at 25% white on the body) or drop the body one.
- [N4] Empty state → footer gap is large (~100px of bare blue at 390, phone-08). Reduce `.footer` top padding when `#empty` is shown, or give the empty card a "⬆ Back to top" pill so the space has a purpose.
- [N5] Match cards could carry the players' thumbnail photos (32px round, ink border) — it would tie the match list to the sticker album and help a kid match faces to names on court.

## What's great (keep)
- The direction lands: court blue + cream sticker cards + ball yellow + ink hard shadows is instantly recognisable and reads as "made for Nora", not a template. Nothing grey, nothing thin; contrast is superb at arm's length.
- Cards are genuinely tactile: 4px border, 6px offset shadow, alternating −1.2°/+1° tilt, the flag sticker and the ball seed badge on the photo, and the star pop animation with the gold fill state. The favorites flow (star → "My favorites" gold pill → "3 of 32 players" → ⭐ next to names in the match list) is clear and satisfying.
- Hero title at 76.8px on tablet with the double text-shadow is a proper poster; the bouncing ball and court lines are charming.
- Detail sheet: the big yellow ✕, the blue "Saturday's match" box with the underlined opponent link, the yellow "Watch for this!" box and the 3D flip reveal are all delightful; prev/next sticky nav is easy for small thumbs.
- Filter pills at 48px tall with the peel wobble and tier-coloured active states are perfectly kid-sized.
- Engineering hygiene: fonts fall back gracefully offline, `file://` works, focus rings are visible on blue and cream, no console errors, no horizontal overflow, reduced-motion path is handled.
